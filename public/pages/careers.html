Why Work With Us?

At Cody Drug, we believe medicine should be built around people, not products, and that belief shapes how we treat our patients, our partners, and our team. We’re growing a pharmacy that earns trust through precision, speed, and personal care. In order to do that, we need a team that thrives together.

Working here means being part of a team that values grit, integrity, urgency, and reliability. We expect accountability, encourage candor, and lead through service by challenging ideas, supporting one another, and doing the right thing even when it’s hard. If you want meaningful work, high standards, and the opportunity to grow alongside people who care deeply about what they do, you might be a great fit for Cody Drug.

About Cody Drug

Since 2010, Cody Drug has been more than just a pharmacy – it’s a place where customers always come first. Founded by Stacy Cody and located in a replica 1950s diner, we combine friendly service with modern care. Our compounding pharmacy offers custom & sterile personalized medications, plus services like vaccinations, telehealth solutions, medication packaging, & wellness supplements.

Safety and Compliance

Our sterile lab is meticulously maintained to comply with the most stringent USP standards, ensuring an ultra-clean environment free from contaminants.

High-Quality Products

Designed to support optimal health by delivering essential nutrients in their most effective and bioavailable forms. Unlike generic options, these products focus on delivering tangible results.

Improved Efficiency and Accuracy

Advanced air filtration systems and controlled access protocols work together to maintain a sterile setting, essential for producing high-quality, safe medications.

Personalized medicine. Precision science. Patient-centered support.
